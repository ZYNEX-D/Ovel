Ovel — Commercial Operating System
Executive Project Proposal & Product Specification
1. Key Value Proposition & Architecture
Effortless Merchant Setup: Instant store initialization with no technical configuration needed; merchants publish their branded link (ovel.app/l/:slug) in under 2 minutes.
Direct Commerce Channel: Showcase services, retail products, custom order requests, verified client reviews, and automated SEO schema markup (LocalBusiness, OpenGraph, Twitter Cards).
Frictionless Client Experience: Clients book appointments, place custom orders, track order/appointment statuses, and leave reviews through a dedicated Client Portal using just their Phone Number and Password.
2. Conversational Client Checkout Flow
When an unauthenticated client clicks to book or purchase, they are guided through a fluid multi-screen modal with smooth slide animations:

[Screen 1: Full Name] 
      └──> [Screen 2: Phone Number (+94 format supported)] 
                 └──> [Screen 3: Create Secure Password] 
                            └──> [Screen 4: "Keep Me Signed In" Toggle] 
                                       └──> [Screen 5: Welcome Greeting + Haptic Confirmation] 
                                                  └──> [Immediate Deposit / Order Lock]
Client Portal: Returning clients sign in with their phone and password to track order progress (Confirmed, Preparing, Ready, Completed), reschedule with buffer rules, download PDF invoices, and submit 1–5 star ratings.
3. Single-Use VIP Pass & Offer Link Engine
Merchant Controls: Generate an exclusive private link with custom pricing, discounted rates, or special unlisted bundle offerings.
Auto-Expiration: Immediately expires once the client confirms their booking or order, preventing link sharing or unauthorized re-use.
